# SOKOSHAMBA

### A Kenyan Agricultural Marketplace and Market Intelligence Platform

**SOKOSHAMBA** is a data-driven digital platform designed to connect farmers, buyers, consumers and agricultural stakeholders across Kenya.

The platform aims to improve agricultural market access, price transparency and decision-making through digital commerce, statistical analysis and market intelligence.

## Objectives

- Connect farmers directly with buyers and consumers.
- Improve transparency of agricultural commodity prices.
- Provide regional market comparisons across Kenya.
- Identify potential supply shortages and market opportunities.
- Analyse agricultural supply and demand.
- Develop predictive models for agricultural prices.
- Incorporate weather information into agricultural decision-making.
- Provide agricultural policy, news and government information.
- Create a future marketplace for agricultural inputs.

## Planned Features

- Farmer registration and produce listings.
- Buyer marketplace and order management.
- Regional agricultural market prices.
- Price gap analysis.
- Supply and demand analysis.
- Price forecasting.
- Shortage and surplus alerts.
- Weather intelligence.
- Government policies and agricultural news.
- Farm input marketplace.
